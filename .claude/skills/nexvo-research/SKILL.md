# NEXVO research

## Procedure
1. Search official sources first: joradp.dz (Journal officiel), ons.dz, ministry sites, AND, UNEP, World Bank.
2. For each finding add a row to `SOURCES.md`: ID, title, publisher, URL, date, access date, quality (A/B/C/U), notes.
3. Add the claim to `RESEARCH-DOSSIER.md` §1 with limitation and the decision it affects.
4. Never invent article numbers, licence names, prices or market sizes. Unknown = "unknown".
5. Legal points sourced only from press/law-firm notes must carry ⚠ in PRIVACY-AND-COMPLIANCE.md.

## Verification
- Every number shown to users or investors traces to a source ID or an assumption ID (A-xx).
- Re-check institutional domains (they change; see S-29).
