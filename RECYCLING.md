**Corrections to the earlier secondary-source summary:** the obligation is *both* to ensure valorisation (art. 7, through eco-organisations) *and* to pay an éco-contribution (art. 7 bis 1) — not "recover **or** pay"; the "national digital system" is a *national information and mapping system for waste data* (art. 5 bis 1); the JO issue is dated **23 February 2025**. Every operational detail (eco-organisations, éco-contribution amounts, end-of-waste criteria, plastics schedule, information-system format) is left **to implementing regulations not yet obtained** → still not assumed by the product.

### 1.5 Décret exécutif 06-104 — waste nomenclature (grade A: primary text, JORA n° 13 of 5 March 2006, pp. 9–61)

Read in full from the PDF supplied by the project owner ([SOURCES S-90](SOURCES.md)).
- **Code** = `category.section.rubrique` (art. 2-A), e.g. `20.3.1`. Each rubric has a **class**: MA (ménagers et assimilés), I (inertes), S (spéciaux), SD (spéciaux dangereux) (art. 2-B); SD rubrics also list **hazard criteria** from annex I (explosible, inflammable, toxique, …) (art. 2-C).
- **Annex II** lists MA and I wastes (63 classified rubrics); **annex III** lists S and SD wastes (705). `.99 Déchets non spécifiés` rubrics carry no class.
- **The same code exists in both annexes with different meanings** — `20.1.1` is "Papier et carton" (MA, annex II) and "Solvants" (SD, annex III); `20.1.21` "Métaux" exists only in annex III as S. A waste is therefore identified by **(code, class)**, never by the code alone.
- Art. 4: the nomenclature applies whether the waste goes to recovery or disposal; being listed does not by itself make a material a waste.
- Useful annex II rubrics for collectors: 20.1.1 Papier et carton · 20.1.2 Verre · 20.1.3 Déchets de cuisine et de cantine biodégradables · 20.1.7 Matières plastiques · 20.2.1 Déchets biodégradables (jardins) · 20.3.1 Déchets communaux en mélange · 20.3.2 Déchets de marchés · 15.1.x packaging by material · 17.x construction waste (I). Note: annex II has **no municipal metals rubric** (metal packaging is 15.1.4 MA; "Métaux" 20.1.21 is S in annex III).

### 1.6 Special hazardous waste — décrets 04-409 and 05-315 (grade A: primary texts, PDFs supplied by the project owner)

**Décret exécutif 04-409 of 14 December 2004 — transport of special hazardous waste (JORA n° 81 of 19 December 2004, pp. 3–5).**
- Definitions (art. 2): *expéditeur* (holds the waste, transports it or hands it over for transport), *transporteur*, *destinataire* (receives it for valorisation or disposal).
- Packaging suited to nature/state/danger (art. 4); types fixed by joint arrêté (art. 5); **legible, indelible labels** identifying the waste (art. 6, label specs by arrêté).
- Vehicles adapted, periodic technical inspection, external signage (art. 7–9); carrier holds a **professional certificate** (art. 10); accident → notify police/gendarmerie, civil protection and local authorities (art. 11).
- **Transport authorisation** required (art. 13, under law 01-19 art. 24); it attests the carrier's habilitation (art. 14); file contents/granting by joint arrêté (art. 15); **valid authorisation on every transport**, shown on inspection (art. 16).
- **Movement document** accompanies every transport to check legal conformity, each operator's intervention **and any refusal by a party**, route and timing (art. 17); its characteristics fixed by an interministerial arrêté (defence, interior, environment, transport) (art. 18) — **not obtained**.
- Carrier may not leave the treatment facility before the container is **cleaned and decontaminated**, at the holder's cost (art. 19); authorities may sample (art. 20); **shipper, carrier and consignee sign** the document (art. 21). Sea/rail by arrêté (art. 22).

**Décret exécutif 05-315 of 10 September 2005 — declaration of special hazardous waste (JORA n° 62 of 11 September 2005, pp. 5–7).**
- The declaration covers nature, quantity, characteristics, treatment and prevention measures, **on the annex form** (art. 2), sent to the environment administration **within three months after the close of the year** (art. 3).
- Annex sections: identification of the generator/holder (status, name, head office, activity, certification, person in charge); **A** nature (raw material, name, **code**, consistency solid/liquid/gaseous/pasty, mixtures), **quantity t/year**, characteristics (chemical composition, **hazard criterion**), storage (temporary/permanent, t/year, method); **B** treatment (management, control, disposal methods, installation types, treatment types, quantities treated t/year, yield); **C** measures — **reuse / recycling / valorisation / elimination t/year**, and five families of measures (minimisation, good practice, available techniques, cleaner production, risk management).
- Same JO: décret 05-314 (approval of groupings of generators/holders of special waste) — noted, not modelled.

### 1.7 Implementing arrêtés, décret 19-10 and law 01-19 (grade A: primary texts supplied by the project owner, 2026-11-01)

**Arrêté interministériel du 26 Chaoual 1434 / 2 septembre 2013 — transport authorisation** (04-409 art. 15; FAOLEX alg200196):
- File (art. 2): request with name/address, **nature, name and code (06-104) of the wastes**, **crew list**, **loading and unloading points and main waypoints**; certified copies of driving licences, **professional certificates**, transport insurance, registration cards, **technical-inspection and conformity reports** of vehicles and trailers, circulation permits, trade register; **planned duration**.
- Granted after checking vehicle conditions (04-409 art. 7–9); **answer within 2 months**; transport ministry opinion within 30 days; refusal reasoned and notified (art. 3). **Decision of the environment minister after the transport minister's opinion** (art. 4). The authorisation fixes the **duration of the transport** (art. 5). Breaches → **withdrawal** (art. 7).
- Annex (characteristics of the authorisation): applicant; **waste names and codes**; **authorised vehicles** (type, make, plate, technical-inspection, conformity and circulation-permit numbers and validity); loading/unloading points and route; **authorised crew** (licence, certificate, insurance numbers); trade register; **validity period**.
- ⇒ the authorisation is **per wastes + vehicles + route + crew + period**, not a company-wide number (D-62).

**Arrêté interministériel du 2 septembre 2013 — labels** (04-409 art. 6; me.gov.dz ART-DEC-04-409-1):
- Label content (art. 3): « déchets spéciaux dangereux »; waste name; **06-104 code**; **hazard criterion**; **risk indication and precautionary advice**; quantity; origin (**name and address of generator/holder and consignee**); destination.
- Must follow the **annex model** and cover **1/10 of the package surface** (art. 4). Annex model is **bilingual Arabic/French**: header, Déchet, Code / Déchet n°, Critère de dangerosité, Quantité (kg), Nom and Adresse (générateur et/ou détenteur / destinataire); **items with an asterisk (header and hazard criterion) printed in red**.

**Décret exécutif 19-10 du 23 janvier 2019 — export of special hazardous waste** (JORA n° 07 of 30 January 2019, pp. 7–15):
- Export only if **no national facility** can treat the waste (art. 3); **authorisation of the environment minister** (art. 4) requested by an **exporter habilitated** by the minister; application file with export contract, **notification document** (valid 12 months, annex II), **movement document** (annex III), physico-chemical analysis by an approved body, **5 % bank guarantee** returned after the last valorisation/elimination certificate, insurance (art. 5). Intersectoral committee (art. 12–13); authorisation valid **12 months** (art. 14); decision within **2 months**, appeal within 15 days (art. 15); copy of the movement document within 3 months and certificate of elimination/valorisation (art. 17); extension up to 6 months (art. 18).
- **Annex III** = Basel-Convention-style movement document (22 boxes: notification no., exporter, importer, actual quantity, packaging, up to 3 carriers with pickup date and signature, generator, installation, D/R operation, waste identification codes incl. Basel/OECD/H/UN, exporter declaration, reception by consignee and installation (accepted/rejected), certificate of elimination/valorisation, customs boxes). **It applies to exports; it is not the domestic movement document** of 04-409 art. 18, which is still not found.
- Annex IV: packaging and labelling for movements — same label mentions as the 2013 arrêté (minus quantity/risk), external vehicle signage, **no mixing with other waste** during transport.
- Export is **not modelled** in NEXVO (rare for collectors; needs ministerial habilitation) — D-63.
- Academic commentary (S-98, grade B) agrees with this reading and states Algeria acceded to the **Basel Convention** with a reservation on 16 May 1998 (to verify against the accession decree before citing). It does not identify the domestic movement-document arrêté either; nor does a December 2025 article on the transport authorisation (S-99), which refers back to the decree. As of 2026-11-01 that arrêté is treated as **not published or not publicly available**.

**Law 01-19 (Arabic original, JORA n° 77 of 15 December 2001)** — scanned copy; pages 12–14 read: art. 15–19 (special waste only in licensed facilities; mixing hazardous with other waste prohibited — art. 17), **art. 21** (declaration to the minister — basis of 05-315), **art. 22** (a licensed facility that refuses special waste must notify the holder in writing with reasons and inform the minister), **art. 24** (transport subject to the environment minister's authorisation after consulting the transport minister), art. 25–28 (import ban, export conditions), art. 29–34 (communal plan; household waste is the commune's responsibility; the commune may delegate collection by specifications to public or private operators — the legal basis of NEXVO Business customers).

### 1.3 Ecosystem

- **AND / SNID**: a national waste information system exists at the AND; its presentation lists local authorities, generators, collectors/transporters, **recyclers/transformers** as information providers, with CSV export ([EIONET presentation](https://eni-seis.eionet.europa.eu/south/communication/events/project-related-events/country-visit-to-algeria/documents/presentation-and)). The AND site has a recycler/collector registration form that asks for **purchase and sale prices of PET at each stage** ([and.dz](https://and.dz/https-snid-and-dz-inscription_recyc_recuppp-php/)). The data format for operators is unknown.
- **Eco-Jem**: packaging-recovery system created by a 2004 decree, financed by member companies, with an AND label and platform ([AlgérieInvest](https://www.algerieinvest.dz/recyclage-des-dechets-demballages-lagence-nationale-des-dechets-lance-le-label-eco-jem/)). No published performance evaluation found.
- **Sorting infrastructure**: 15 (minister, 2023) vs 23 (press) sorting centres; few operational; waste arrives mixed and late because there is **no source separation** ([Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/38508), [Dzair World](https://www.dzairworld.com/2021/06/24/tri-dechets-menagers-algerie/)).
- **Informal collectors** set most prices; they prefer clean homogeneous rigid plastics, PET and film ([ASJP study](https://asjp.cerist.dz/en/downArticle/9/17/1/146374)). Classified ads show scattered prices (PET 15 DA/kg wanted; cardboard bales 15,000 DA/t; aluminium cans 90,000 DA/t — undated, unverified) ([EspaceAgro](https://www.espaceagro.com/dechet-carton-algerie/exp-dechet-carton-algerie.html)). **There is no reliable public price series** → NEXVO must not show market prices it cannot source.

## 3. Proposed module "NEXVO Recycle" (phased)

### Phase R1 — Source-separated collection and proof of recovery (fits current customers)
- **Material streams** (cardboard/paper, PET, HDPE, film, metals, aluminium, glass, organic, residual) mapped to waste categories; contract lines and work orders per stream.
- **Weight per stream** on each work order (several weight tickets, one per material) with photos; contamination flag.
- **Destination**: where each load went (sorting centre, named recycler, landfill/CET) — chain of custody.
- **Diversion rate** per customer, per site, per month and per commune = recovered ÷ total, always traceable to the tickets.
- **Recovery certificate (attestation de valorisation)** per customer and period: tonnes per material, destination recycler, references — what businesses will need to show they meet a "recover your waste" obligation **once the decrees confirm it**.

### Phase R2 — Sorting yard / centre de tri (for companies that sort and sell)
- **Inbound**: own collections + **purchases from collectors** (registered sellers, weight, grade, price per kg entered by the operator, paid with a printed/receipted voucher).
- **Sorting and baling**: bales with ID/QR label, material, grade, weight; rejects and losses recorded (mass balance in vs out).
- **Inventory** by material, grade and location.
- **Sales to recyclers**: buyer registry (with their registration/approval number), operator-set price lists, outbound weight ticket, delivery note, invoice, quality claims, **margin per material**.

### Phase R3 — Municipal and national reporting
- Commune dashboards: tonnes by stream, diversion rate vs the 30 %/2029 target, sorting campaigns, drop-off points (apport volontaire) with IoT fill levels.
- **SNID/AND export** once the AND data format is obtained (contact the AND; do not guess the format).
- EPR producer declarations / éco-contribution support **only after the implementing decrees** are published.

### Gated (not before evidence)
- **NEXVO Market** (B2B exchange between sorters and recyclers) — needs supply/demand evidence and a settlement model (existing roadmap gate).
- **Eco Points** for citizens/collectors — needs an approved, costed, abuse-resistant policy (D-14).
- Public price indices — no reliable source exists.

## 6. What is implemented (Phase R1, 2026-11-01)

- **Material stream** on every weight ticket (defaulted from the work-order category, changeable — several tickets per job, one per material), **contamination flag**, **processor rejects** (≤ net weight, audited).
- **Destinations** registry (recycler, sorting centre, composting, landfill/CET, incineration, other) with approval number; a ticket can only point to an active destination of the same organization.
- **Recovery report** (`/app/recycling`): rate = recovered ÷ (recovered + disposed + untracked), by month, material, destination and customer; Excel/CSV ledger export at ticket level. Rules in DECISIONS D-54.
- **Recovery certificate** (attestation de valorisation) per customer and month, printable, also in the customer portal for the customer's own data; lists every ticket and the method.
- **Category classification** with stream, regulatory class and a décret 06-104 code **validated against the decree for that class** (D-55); the demo seed uses 20.3.1 / 20.1.1 / 20.1.2.
- **Law 25-02 alignment (2026-11-01, second pass):** destination kinds add anaerobic digestion, reuse/repair and energy recovery; recovery = material **or** energy valorisation as defined in art. 3, energy shown separately; incineration without energy recovery and landfill = disposal; indicator for organic waste outside composting/digestion (art. 35 bis); special waste needs a destination with a recorded approval/licence number (art. 15, 19).
- **Special hazardous waste (2026-11-01, third and fourth passes):** transport authorisations recorded as granted — wastes (SD codes), named vehicles, route, crew, validity, withdrawal — and every transport must use one that is valid that day, covers its code and names its vehicle (04-409 art. 13–16, arrêté du 2 septembre 2013); bilingual package labels per the label arrêté's annex model (one per package, starred items in red); planned D/R operation and estimated/weighed quantity at creation; reception with net received, refused part and reason (law 01-19 art. 22); treatment confirmed with the D/R code actually carried out, date and facility signatory (décret 19-10 art. 17; codes from its annexes) — field coverage benchmarked against the French BSDD, which has no force in Algeria (S-100); transports with SD code and hazard criteria copied from 06-104, consistency, packaging, labels required before pickup (art. 6), shipper + carrier signatures at pickup, consignee signature only with decontamination confirmed (art. 19), refusal by any party recorded (art. 17), licensed consignee only (law art. 15/19), printable; DB CHECK constraints mirror the rules. **Annual declaration (05-315)** pre-filled per customer and year in the annex's section order — code, designation, hazard criterion, t/year, installations, section C split — with blanks for what only the generator knows and the due date (31 March of the next year). Clearly labelled as internal tracking: the official movement-document form (04-409 art. 18) is not available.
- AI assistant tool `get_recycling_summary` (read-only, reports.read).
- Not done (by design, D-56): prices, éco-contribution, national information-system export, hazard-criteria handling for SD waste tracking (décrets 04-409 / 05-315 not yet read).
